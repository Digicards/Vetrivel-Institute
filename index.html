About Me/Us

Vetrivel Institute is a leading online education platform and organization dedicated to Education and Skill Development, founded by Gomathi V.S. (Propreitor) We are committed to fostering professional excellence and personal growth through a dual-focus approach:

Education: We provide rigorous, focused training for high-stakes competitive examinations, including Judicial Services, TNPSC, Banking, AIBE, CLAT, CJ, DJ, Drafting courses for Advocates and so on,

Skill Development: We offer a diverse range of vocational and personal enrichment programs, such as Yoga & Healing, Reflexology, Aari Embriodiary, Voice Dubbing, Life Coaching, Communication Skills and so on,

Commitment to Quality: Vetrivel Institute operates under the highest standards of accountability and excellence, demonstrated by our registration under MSME and our ISO 9001:2015 certification. We strive to empower every student and professional to achieve their full potential.
